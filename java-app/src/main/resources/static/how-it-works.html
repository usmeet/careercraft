HOW IT WORKS

How to decode a job description, and how CareerCraft AI does it for you

Decoding a job description means separating what the employer truly needs from the wishlist and the filler. CareerCraft AI follows that same approach, and every one of its tools works in the same simple way.

From paste to result in four steps

1

You choose a tool.

Pick the JD Decoder, Resume Enhancer, LinkedIn Builder, Culture Analyzer, or Interview Simulator.

2

You paste your text.

A job posting, a resume bullet, a short description of your background, or a company's values.

3

The tool applies its checks.

For a job posting, that means looking for the must-have skills and the warning signs in the wording. For a resume bullet, it means stronger action verbs and the XYZ structure. Each tool has its own set of steps built for its job.

4

You get a clear result.

It comes back organized and ready to use, so you can copy it into your application or practice with it.

What it can and can't do

  • It's guidance, not a guarantee. CareerCraft AI helps you prepare. It can't promise interviews or offers.
  • It combines live Gemini AI with deterministic rules. We use state-of-the-art LLM intelligence for nuanced semantic analysis, backed by a deterministic rule engine for reliability. Always review suggestions and align them with your authentic experience.
  • The Culture Analyzer works from what you give it. For now it uses company values that you paste in, instead of looking companies up itself.
  • You stay in charge. Check every result against your own real experience before using it.

Your privacy

  • If you're logged in, each request and its result is saved to your private history so you can come back to it.
  • If you're a guest, nothing you paste is saved.
