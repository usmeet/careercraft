# Your job search in one place, not five tabs

CareerCraft AI is an AI career assistant for job seekers. Instead of hopping between tools to decode job postings, rewrite your resume, fix your LinkedIn, and prepare for interviews, you do it all here, and each result is ready to use in an application.

**[Try the demo]** (primary button, goes to `/try`)

## Why this exists

Job hunting is scattered. Most people end up juggling five or more separate tools just to get one application ready. That tool fatigue costs time, and generic advice from one tab rarely matches what the next tab says. CareerCraft AI puts the key steps in one place so you can go from a messy job posting to application-ready material in seconds.

## Five tools, one assistant

### JD Decoder
Job postings are messy. Paste one in and the JD Decoder pulls out the must-have technical skills and flags the hidden red flags you might miss on a first read.
*Example:* "Decode this JD for a fresher: We need a Python developer with REST API and cloud experience."

### Resume Enhancer
Weak bullets say what you did. Strong ones say what you achieved. The Resume Enhancer swaps vague wording for strong action verbs and reshapes bullets with the XYZ formula: accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z.
*Example:* "Improve this bullet: Worked on a website for our college fest."

### LinkedIn Builder
Recruiters search before they message. The LinkedIn Builder writes search-friendly headlines designed to help you show up and earn more recruiter InMail.
*Example:* "Write a LinkedIn headline for a final-year engineering student interested in backend development."

### Culture Analyzer
Interviews go both ways. Paste in a company's stated values and the Culture Analyzer suggests reverse-interviewing questions, so you can find out whether the place fits you.
*Example:* "Company values: customer obsession, ownership, bias for action. What should I ask in my interview?"

### Interview Simulator
Not every round is the same. The Interview Simulator sorts practice questions into Behavioral, Technical, and Case-based rounds, so you prepare for the round you will actually face.
*Example:* "Give me interview questions for a junior Java developer."

## 3. How It Works (`/how-it-works`)

**Page title:** How to Decode a Job Description: How CareerCraft AI Works
**Meta description:** See how CareerCraft AI turns a pasted job description, resume bullet, or company value list into a result, in plain steps, with what it can and can't do.

# How to decode a job description, and how CareerCraft AI does it for you

Decoding a job description means separating what the employer truly needs from the wishlist and the filler. CareerCraft AI follows that same approach, and every one of its tools works in the same simple way.

## From paste to result in four steps

1. **You choose a tool.** Pick the JD Decoder, Resume Enhancer, LinkedIn Builder, Culture Analyzer, or Interview Simulator.
2. **You paste your text.** A job posting, a resume bullet, a short description of your background, or a company's values.
3. **The tool applies its checks.** For a job posting, that means looking for the must-have skills and the warning signs in the wording. For a resume bullet, it means stronger action verbs and the XYZ structure. Each tool has its own set of steps built for its job.
4. **You get a clear result.** It comes back organized and ready to use, so you can copy it into your application or practice with it.

## What it can and can't do

- **It's guidance, not a guarantee.** CareerCraft AI helps you prepare. It can't promise interviews or offers.
- **It uses rules and templates, not a live AI model.** That keeps results consistent, but it also means it won't understand every unusual posting or industry. Treat the output as a strong first draft and edit it.
- **The Culture Analyzer works from what you give it.** For now it uses company values that you paste in, instead of looking companies up itself.
- **You stay in charge.** Check every result against your own real experience before using it.

## Your privacy

- **If you're logged in,** each request and its result is saved to your private history so you can come back to it.
- **If you're a guest,** nothing you paste is saved.

# About CareerCraft AI

CareerCraft AI is a career assistant for job seekers. It was built by **Manasvi Sawant**, and the concept comes from her Google Cloud Gen AI Academy (APAC Edition) project. The aim is simple: replace the five-plus separate tools a job seeker juggles with one assistant that turns generic advice into application-ready material.

## How it was built

This version is a Java web application, built as a mini project for the Advanced Java Programming Lab course (Savitribai Phule Pune University). It uses Spring Boot, Hibernate, and MySQL.
